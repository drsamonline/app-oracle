Oracle

A compliment built around one real detail, and a small myth built from what a photo actually shows. Offline — nothing is uploaded.

about

One real detail

This app will not produce a generic compliment and call it specific. Give it one thing you actually noticed about the person — what they did, how they did it — and it builds the compliment around that, verbatim.

No detail, no compliment. That is the whole rule.

    It reads colour, light and composition — and nothing else. It will not tell you who is in the photo, how old they are, what they feel, or anything about them at all. The myth it writes is invented, and it says so. The photo is never kept.
